General & First Appointments

Your 45-minute first visit includes a detailed medical and lifestyle history review, high-resolution multispectral skin imaging, a physical clinical evaluation by your board-certified dermatologist, and a written personalized diagnosis and treatment plan with honest cost estimates.

Please arrive with a clean face free of heavy makeup, foundation, or tinted moisturizers. Bring a list or photos of all current skincare products, prescriptions, and supplements you are taking.

We request at least 24 hours notice for rescheduling or cancellation. Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours notice may incur a $50 administrative fee.

Treatments, Pain & Downtime

Patient comfort is our priority. For fractional lasers and deeper peels, we apply a high-potency medical topical anesthetic cream 30–45 minutes prior to the procedure. Most patients describe treatments as mild warmth or a rubber band snap feeling.

Fractional laser resurfacing typically requires 3 to 5 days of social downtime, during which you will experience mild redness and microscopic flaking. Non-ablative laser toning and HydraFacial have virtually zero downtime.

HydraFacials provide immediate glow after a single session. For deeper acne scarring or pigment correction, a series of 3 to 6 sessions spaced 4 weeks apart is standard for optimal, long-term collagen remodeling.

Safety, Hygiene & Standards

All laser resurfacing, injectables, and medical diagnoses are performed exclusively by board-certified medical doctors. Advanced clinical facials are administered by licensed medical aestheticians under direct physician supervision.
