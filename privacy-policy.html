Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At Aurelia Dermatology & Skin Clinic ("Aurelia", "we", "our", or "us"), we are committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of our patients' personal and medical data. This Privacy Policy outlines our standards for collecting, using, and safeguarding your information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly through our website booking system, contact forms, or clinic intake forms, including: full name, contact telephone, email address, appointment preferences, past dermatological treatment history, and medical health records necessary for safe clinical evaluation.

2. Use of Information

Your information is used exclusively to: schedule consultations and procedures, send SMS and email appointment reminders, maintain medical patient charts, process billing, and provide post-procedure aftercare instructions.

3. Medical Confidentiality & HIPAA

Protected Health Information (PHI) is handled strictly according to HIPAA guidelines and relevant medical privacy standards. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or health data to third-party advertisers under any circumstances.

4. Contacting Our Privacy Officer

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request copies of your health records, please email privacy@aureliaclinic.com or write to Aurelia Dermatology, 123 Wellness Avenue, Suite 200.
