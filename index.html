FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions.

You can book an appointment online through our website, call us at (555) 123-4567, or send us a WhatsApp message. Our team will confirm your appointment within 24 hours.

Please bring a valid ID, any relevant medical records, a list of current medications and skincare products you use, and any previous treatment records related to your skin concern.

A typical first consultation takes 30–45 minutes. This includes a detailed skin assessment, discussion of your concerns and goals, and development of a personalized treatment plan.

During your consultation, our dermatologist will assess your skin, discuss your concerns and recommend the most appropriate treatment options. We encourage you to ask questions and be part of the decision-making process.

Yes, all our treatments are performed by qualified, experienced dermatologists using approved equipment and products. We follow strict safety protocols and will discuss any potential risks during your consultation.

The number of sessions depends on the treatment type and your individual skin condition. Your dermatologist will provide a clear treatment plan during your consultation, including the expected number of sessions.

Downtime varies by treatment. Non-invasive treatments like HydraFacial typically have no downtime, while treatments like chemical peels or laser may require a few days of recovery. We'll provide detailed aftercare instructions.

Results timelines vary by treatment and individual skin response. Some treatments show immediate improvement, while others may take several weeks. Your dermatologist will set realistic expectations during your consultation.
