Biography & Background

Dr. Sophia Laurent is a board-certified dermatologist and the Medical Director of Aurelia Dermatology. With over 14 years of clinical experience, Dr. Laurent has treated thousands of patients suffering from complex acne, stubborn melasma, hormonal pigmentation, and skin aging.

She completed her medical degree with honors from Johns Hopkins University School of Medicine, followed by her dermatology residency at Stanford University Medical Center. Her research has been featured in leading dermatology journals including the Journal of Investigative Dermatology and JAAD.

Clinical Philosophy

"I believe that healthy skin is the true foundation of radiant beauty. Rather than chasing superficial short-term trends, my approach is rooted in understanding each patient's individual cellular biology, skin barrier health, and lifestyle."

Barrier-First Protocol
Ensuring the protective lipid barrier is healthy before aggressive treatments.
Minimal Invasiveness
Opting for progressive, sustainable skin restoration over aggressive trauma.

Key Areas of Expertise

  • Hormonal Acne & Severe Breakouts: Multi-modality protocols targeting inflammation and sebum control.
  • Melasma & Post-Inflammatory Hyperpigmentation: Gentle laser toning, prescription topicals, and medical peels.
  • Facial Rejuvenation & Skin Quality: HydraFacial, skin boosters, and collagen induction therapies.
  • Rosacea & Vascular Sensitivity: Soothing vascular calming treatments and customized barrier support.
