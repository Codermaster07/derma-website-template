Acne & Breakouts

Acne is one of the most common skin conditions, affecting people of all ages. It can present as blackheads, whiteheads, pimples, cysts or nodules. Our dermatologists offer personalized treatment plans that may help manage acne and reduce its impact on your skin and confidence.

Understanding Acne

Acne occurs when hair follicles become clogged with oil and dead skin cells. It can be influenced by hormones, genetics, diet, stress and environmental factors. While often associated with adolescence, acne can affect adults well into their 30s, 40s and beyond.

Types of Acne

  • Comedonal Acne — Blackheads and whiteheads caused by clogged pores
  • Inflammatory Acne — Red, swollen pimples and pustules
  • Cystic Acne — Deep, painful cysts beneath the skin surface
  • Hormonal Acne — Breakouts often linked to hormonal changes
  • Adult Acne — Acne that persists or develops in adulthood

Treatment Options

Treatment depends on the type and severity of acne. Our dermatologists may recommend:

  • Topical treatments (retinoids, benzoyl peroxide, antibiotics)
  • Oral medications where appropriate
  • Chemical peels to unclog pores and reduce breakouts
  • HydraFacial for deep cleansing
  • Light and laser therapy
  • Lifestyle and skincare guidance

When to See a Dermatologist

You should consider consulting a dermatologist if you have persistent acne that doesn't respond to over-the-counter products, if your acne is leaving scars, or if it's significantly affecting your self-confidence or daily life.
