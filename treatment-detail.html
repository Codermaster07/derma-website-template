Facial Treatment

HydraFacial

The HydraFacial is a non-invasive, multi-step facial treatment that may help cleanse, exfoliate, extract, and hydrate your skin. Using patented technology, this treatment is often used to address a variety of skin concerns including fine lines, enlarged pores, dullness, and uneven skin texture.

Duration 30–45 min
Sessions 4–6 recommended
Downtime None
Book This Treatment

Who Is It For?

HydraFacial may be suitable for most skin types and can be customized to address specific concerns. It is often recommended for individuals looking to improve overall skin health, hydration, and radiance. Common concerns it may help address include:

  • Dull, tired-looking skin
  • Fine lines and early signs of aging
  • Enlarged pores and congestion
  • Uneven skin tone or texture
  • Dehydrated skin
  • Oily or acne-prone skin

Benefits

  • Deep cleansing and gentle exfoliation
  • Extraction of impurities without irritation
  • Intense hydration with specialized serums
  • Improvement in skin tone and texture over time
  • No downtime — can often return to normal activities immediately
  • Suitable for most skin types

How It Works

The HydraFacial treatment typically uses a patented device that combines cleansing, exfoliation, extraction, and hydration steps in a single treatment session. The process involves:

  • Step 1 — Cleanse & Peel: Gentle exfoliation and resurfacing to uncover a new layer of skin.
  • Step 2 — Extract & Hydrate: Painless suction to remove debris from pores while nourishing with intense moisturizers.
  • Step 3 — Fuse & Protect: Saturation of the skin's surface with antioxidants and peptides.

Treatment Process

Your treatment begins with a brief consultation to assess your skin and customize the treatment. The procedure itself typically takes 30–45 minutes and is generally described as comfortable and relaxing. Results may be visible immediately after the treatment.

Recovery & Aftercare

One of the key advantages of HydraFacial is that it typically requires no downtime. You may return to your normal activities immediately. Your dermatologist will provide aftercare recommendations including sunscreen application and skincare guidance.

Expected Results

Many patients report seeing an improvement in skin radiance and hydration after a single session. For optimal results, a series of treatments is often recommended. Results may vary depending on individual skin condition and treatment plan.
